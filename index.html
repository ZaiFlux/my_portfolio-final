About Journey Education Fun Facts

Im Rodhel N. Condicion, a second-year Computer Science student with a passion for Cloud Engineering. I am deeply interested in this field because it involves managing, maintaining, deploying, scaling, and automating systems to ensure that they run smoothly. As a student, I continuously expand my knowledge and explore Cloud Computing through personal and school projects. Through consistency and determination, I aim to become a Cloud Engineer who can contribute effective solutions to the modern cloud computing industry.

My journey began when I started studying Computer Science. I initially chose this path because I was interested in becoming a Software Engineer, and I was fascinated by the idea of working across both the front end and back end of an application. However, as I explored the field further, I realized that this role did not align with the kind of work I wanted to pursue, so I continued exploring different areas of IT. I eventually discovered Backend Development because I enjoyed understanding how systems work behind the scenes, but as I went deeper into backend programming, I realized that I was not as interested in programming itself as I was in understanding and managing the systems that support applications. This led me to explore Cloud Computing and eventually discover Cloud Engineering, where I became fascinated by how infrastructure, automation, deployment, and scalability work together to keep modern systems running efficiently. The more I learned about cloud technology, the more interested I became in how it is transforming the way businesses build, deploy, and manage their applications. Today, as a college student, I am continuously strengthening my Cloud Engineering skills by learning about automation, deployment, provisioning, and infrastructure management while working with technologies such as Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, and Git/GitHub. I also apply what I learn by building cloud-native projects that allow me to turn concepts into practical experience. As I continue my studies, I aim to solidify my Cloud Engineering skills, gain deeper hands-on experience, and eventually contribute practical solutions to real-world problems in the cloud industry.
